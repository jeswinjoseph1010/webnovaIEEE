ℹ️ About Us

Who We Are

Pioneering technology culture on campus since our founding chapter inauguration.

The IEEE Computer Society Student Branch Chapter is the student arm of the world's leading professional organization for computing. We operate under the umbrella of the Institute of Electrical and Electronics Engineers (IEEE), connecting students with a global network of over 400,000 members across 160+ countries.

Our chapter is dedicated to fostering technical excellence, professional development, and collaborative innovation among students. We organize hackathons, ideathons, coding contests, expert sessions, and industry visits — giving members hands-on exposure beyond the classroom.

🎯 Our Mission

To empower students with cutting-edge technical skills, industry connections, and leadership opportunities through impactful events and community-driven learning.

🔭 Our Vision

To be a nationally recognized student chapter that shapes the next generation of computing professionals, researchers, and innovators.
